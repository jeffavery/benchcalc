How to read a 4-band resistor

Start at the end opposite the separated tolerance band. The first two colors give the digits, the third multiplies them, and the fourth gives the tolerance.

47 × 100 Ω = 4.7 kΩ

The range shows nominal resistance ± tolerance. It does not describe power rating, temperature behavior, or the actual measured resistance.
